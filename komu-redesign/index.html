    About KOMU

    A state university with a mandate open to everyone.

    KOMU exists to advance learning in Imo State and across Nigeria, and to offer higher education to all, regardless of race, creed or sex.

    “Developing the complete man: soul, body and divinity.”
    Vision
    To be a globally competitive University renowned for innovative research and scholarship, world-class education and excellent human resource development.
    Mission
    To make KOMU famous for producing talented men and women with passion and skills to apply knowledge and technology sensibly to solve the problems of the nation and the global community at large.
    Philosophy
    Scholars with the triple capacity for success, global competitiveness and stardom, anchored on our core values.
    Motto
    Selfless Service to Humanity.
    Charter, mandate and objectives

    Establishment

    Kingsley Ozumba (KO) Mbadiwe University was established on 18 February 2021 by the Kingsley Ozumba (KO) Mbadiwe University (Establishment) Law No. 1 of 2021 of Imo State, as a multi-campus university with its main campus at Ogboko, Ideato South LGA. The law repealed the Eastern Palm University (Establishment) Law No. 13 of 2016. Eastern Palm University had been recognised by the NUC as the 42nd state university in Nigeria and the 143rd in the Nigerian university system.

    Mandate

    “To encourage the advancement of learning in Imo State in particular and Nigeria in general, to hold out to all persons without the distinction of race, creed or sex and to give them the opportunity of acquiring higher education in various fields of study.”

    Objectives

    1. Provide a wide range of quality learning opportunities for undergraduate and postgraduate students, without distinction of race, creed, sex, religion or political conviction.
    2. Provide academic, professional and vocational training of high quality that improves state, national and international human resource capabilities.
    3. Foster academic research that contributes to human knowledge and the vitality of the institution.
    4. Encourage thoughtful and responsible staff and student participation in local, state, national and international affairs.
    5. Maintain the highest academic standards for University degrees and other awards, and protect their reputation and integrity.
    6. Evolve academic programmes to suit the changing social and economic needs of society through continuous review of curricula.
    7. Advance human culture and improve human life through the development, refinement and dissemination of knowledge.
    8. Relate its activities to the social, cultural and economic needs of the people of Imo State and Nigeria.

    Our history

    1. 2016

      Eastern Palm University is established under Imo State Law No. 13 of 2016, recognised by the NUC as Nigeria’s 42nd state university.

    2. Feb 2021

      Imo State Law No. 1 of 2021 creates Kingsley Ozumba Mbadiwe University, with its main campus at Ogboko.

    3. 2022

      All four programmes presented earn full NUC accreditation. The JUPEB programme follows in 2023.

    4. Oct 2023

      The public lecture series begins with Prof. Chiedu Mafiana, former Deputy Executive Secretary of the NUC.

    5. Nov 2024

      TD Africa and Huawei launch an ICT Academy with KOMU.

    6. Jan 2026

      Synchronised online classes link the Student Cinema and Senate Chambers.

    7. Aug 2026

      Prof. Frances Ngozi Chukwukere becomes the second substantive Vice-Chancellor.
