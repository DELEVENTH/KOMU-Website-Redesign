How to apply

  1. Check the criteriaConfirm the cut-off, UTME subjects and first-choice rules for your programme.
  2. Start an applicationOpen the form, validate your JAMB registration number and note your application number.
  3. Fill and submitYour JAMB number, date of birth and state must match CAPS.
  4. Pay ₦2,000 on RemitaCard, transfer, USSD, bank invoice or Remita wallet.
  5. Print your slipUse “Continue previous admission process” to return and print your acknowledgement slip.
